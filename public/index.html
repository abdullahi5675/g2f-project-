The Problem We Are Solving

The current paper-based system in Gwaram LGA has four major failures

👻

Ghost Beneficiaries

28–44% of fertilizer allocations could not be traced to verified active farmers. Fake names on the list collect bags meant for real farmers.

📋

No Audit Trail

Paper records can't be queried in real-time. Officials have no way to know who collected what, when, and where during distribution.

⏰

Chronic Delays

Distribution starts 4–6 weeks after registration, consistently missing the optimal planting window — reducing crop yields by 12–17%.

📵

Farmers Not Notified

Farmers travel to multiple distribution points without knowing if their allocation is ready, wasting precious time during planting season.

How the G2F System Fixes This

Five digital modules working together to guarantee transparency

🪪

NIN-Based Registration

Every farmer is registered with their National Identification Number, preventing duplicates and ghost beneficiaries from the start.

📱

Digital QR Vouchers

Each farmer gets a unique QR-coded voucher tied to their identity. It can only be redeemed once, by the right person, at the right point.

📡

USSD Agent Interface

Distribution agents confirm collections using any basic phone — no internet needed. Works even in areas with no data connection.

📊

Real-Time Dashboard

Government officials see live data — who collected, who hasn't, fraud alerts, and full audit logs exportable as reports.

💬

SMS Notifications

Farmers receive SMS alerts when their voucher is ready, when collection opens, and confirmation after they collect — in English and Hausa.

🔄
How the System Works — Step by Step
Step 1 →  LGA Enrolment Officer registers farmer using NIN. System verifies identity and saves farm details.
Step 2 →  System automatically computes bags allocated based on farm size. A unique QR voucher is generated and sent to the farmer by SMS.
Step 3 →  Farmer receives SMS with voucher code. They go to their assigned distribution point within the collection window.
Step 4 →  Distribution Agent dials USSD code, enters farmer's voucher, confirms identity, and records collection. System updates in real time.
Step 5 →  Farmer receives SMS confirmation. Supervisor sees completed transaction on dashboard. Full audit trail is saved automatically.
